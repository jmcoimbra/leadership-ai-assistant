# Leadership Growth Principles

**Research:** 140+ CEO interviews, 14,000-person LinkedIn survey, 7M+ people represented, 35 industries, 20 countries.

---

## Part 1: The State of Leadership

### Ch 1 — The Leadership Gap
- 87.5M-437.5M leaders needed globally by 2030
- 80% of employees say managers are unnecessary
- Only 14% of organizations have a strong leadership bench
- First-time manager age: 30. First leadership training age: 42. 12-year gap
- Notable Nine introduced as the framework

### Ch 2 — Three Essential Leadership Questions
- Q1: What does leadership mean? (Vision + people first)
- Q2: Leader vs manager? (Vision/inspire vs execute/optimize)
- Q3: What are your leadership filters? (Mental models for decisions)
- Case: Ballmer (execution manager) vs Nadella (vision leader). Microsoft market cap $300B → $2.5T+
- Leadership must be defined before it can be practiced

### Ch 3 — The Impact of a Leader
- Toxic leaders cause clinical depression in direct reports
- Good leaders double profits
- Trickle-down effect: leader behavior cascades 3-4 levels
- 13x outperformance with high-quality leaders
- Leader impact is measurable, not aspirational

## Part 2: The 6 Trends

### Ch 4 — AI and Technology
- #1 trend shaping future leadership
- 6 Trends diagram: AI/Tech, Pace of Change, New Talent, Purpose/Meaning, Morality/Ethics/Transparency, Globalization
- AI as partner not replacement
- Accenture: AI augments consultants, 40%+ productivity gains
- McDonald's: acquired Dynamic Yield for AI menu optimization
- Every leader must understand AI capabilities

### Ch 5 — Pace of Change
- S&P 500 lifespan: 60yrs (1950s) → 14yrs (2026)
- 85% of 2030 jobs not invented yet
- Only 53 of original Fortune 500 remain
- Adaptability is survival, not a nice-to-have

### Ch 6 — Purpose and Meaning
- Job → Purpose → Impact → Meaning progression
- 9/10 workers trade money for meaning (23% of earnings)
- Unilever: purpose workshop for 34,000 employees. Purpose brands grew 69% faster
- 85% of purpose-led companies show positive growth vs 42% non-purpose-led

### Ch 7 — New Talent Landscape
- 85M global talent shortage by 2030 ($8.5T unrealized revenue)
- Lifetime employment → lifetime employability
- PwC Digital Fitness app: 275,000 employees assessed
- Gender-diverse exec teams: 21% higher profitability (McKinsey)
- 5-6 generations in workforce simultaneously

### Ch 8 — Morality, Ethics, and Transparency
- Ethics = external rules. Morals = internal compass
- Best Buy: Hubert Joly ethical turnaround. Revenue $40B → $43B in 3 years
- Only 17% say leaders tell truth consistently
- 86% of millennials prioritize ethical employers

## Part 3: The 4 Mindsets

### Ch 9-12 — The Explorer
- Curiosity + humility + vulnerability + perpetual learning
- Curiosity drives 34% more innovation (HBR)
- Humility creates psychological safety
- Vulnerability = birthplace of innovation (Brene Brown)
- Perpetual learning: weekly time outside your domain

### Ch 13 — The Chef
- Strategy + timing + experimentation + purpose
- Balance competing priorities like ingredients
- Run small bets before full commitment
- "Tasting menu" approach for stakeholder feedback

### Ch 14 — The Servant
- Service-oriented: leader exists to serve the team
- Human-centered orgs: 3x employee engagement
- Create environments for people to do best work

### Ch 15 — The Global Citizen
- Multicultural competence non-negotiable for distributed teams
- L'Oreal: international experience managers = 20% more innovative products
- Euro Disney: $175M loss from cultural ignorance (no wine, American-only food)
- Cross-cultural input required for global decisions

### Ch 16 — Mindsets Survey
- 69% employees self-rate high
- Only 6% of managers rate reports "very well"
- Seniority Curse: more senior = more inflated self-perception
- Brazil leads globally in self-assessment

## Part 4: The 5 Skills

### Ch 17 — The Futurist
- #1 CEO-ranked skill
- Cone of Possibilities: Probable → Plausible → Possible → Preposterous
- 4 questions: (1) Assumptions? (2) What changes them? (3) Signals of change? (4) What if opposite?
- Leaders must explore beyond "probable" zone

### Ch 18 — Yoda (Emotional Intelligence)
- EI = Empathy + Self-Awareness (CEO core) + Self-Regulation + Intrinsic Motivation + Social Skills (Goleman)
- 95% think they are self-aware; 10-15% actually are (Eurich)
- Internal self-awareness: values, passions, thoughts
- External self-awareness: how others perceive you
- 87% of CEOs: financial performance tied to empathy
- 90% of top performers high in EI. 20% of bottom performers
- Sanofi: EI training = 13% sales performance increase
- Motorola: EI training = 90% manufacturing productivity increase
- High EI leaders earn $29,000 more per year
- EI behaviors: listen > talk, emphasize how/why over what, recognize contributions, resolve disagreements openly
- Blue Shield CEO: weekly self-reflection, mission statement as guide
- Frances Hesselbein: "Leadership is how to be, not how to do"
- Indra Nooyi (PepsiCo): wrote 400 letters to parents of executives. "I value you as a person"

### Ch 19 — The Translator
- #2 CEO-ranked skill
- Listening ≠ hearing. Listening = purposeful effort
- 58% say worst manager trait: "does not communicate clear expectations"
- 69% of managers: "communication in general" is hardest
- Communication barriers: $62.4M/year lost productivity (Holmes)
- Effective communicator orgs: 47% higher shareholder return over 5 years
- 6 Levels of Listening (Zenger/Folkman):
  1. Create safe environment
  2. Remove distractions
  3. Understand main focus
  4. Observe nonverbal cues (80% of communication)
  5. Understand emotions
  6. Ask questions that challenge assumptions
- Keep/Stop/Start exercise (Charlie Young, Coldwell Banker)
- Communication IS leadership (Melissa Reiff, Container Store)
- Nick Nagano (Tokio Marine CEO): 70% of time communicating. 32,000 workforce hears him 20 min/year. Those minutes must count

### Ch 20 — The Coach
- John Wooden: 10 NCAA championships, Pyramid of Success (15 blocks)
- 3 Ways to Unlock Human Potential: Push (Motivate) + Sustain (Engage) + Pull (Inspire)
- 90th percentile coaches: 88th percentile employee commitment
- 10th percentile coaches: 15th percentile commitment
- Motivating: know what each person values. Only 20% say performance is managed to motivate them
- Engaging: 85% of global workforce disengaged. $7T lost productivity annually
- Employee experience: Technology (30%) + Physical Space (30%) + Culture (40%)
- Inspired employees 2x productive as satisfied
- Creating future leaders: Finkelstein "superboss" research. Top-10 in any industry: >50% worked for same leader
- Google Project Aristotle: #1 = psychological safety. Then: dependability, structure/clarity, meaning, impact
- Amazon: two-pizza rule + single-threaded leader
- Hackman's 6 enabling conditions: real team, compelling direction, enabling structure, supportive context, competent coaching, shared mindset
- Working across generations: 87% say multigenerational workforce increases innovation
- Diverse teams: 60% better results (Forbes). 33% more likely industry-leading profitability (McKinsey)
- Average first-time manager: 30. First training: 42. Like coaching athletes in their 40s
- Enel: "From Leader to Coach" program. 1,300 managers + 7,000 HR supervisors. Created certified internal coaches
- Bain: "Tiger Team" coaching hotline. Manny Maceda (CEO) coaches employees, created response team for scale

### Ch 21 — Technology Teenager
- 9.27 connected devices per person by 2030
- Every company is a technology company
- "Learning by doing" approach to technology adoption
- Leaders do not need to be engineers but must be fluent

### Ch 22 — Skills Survey
- 70% of employees self-rate skills as high
- Only 8% of managers rate reports "very well"
- Yoda (EI) rated hardest skill to develop
- Confirms Seniority Curse pattern

## Part 5: From Knowing to Doing

### Ch 23 — Knowing vs. Doing
- Golden Triangle: Beliefs → Thoughts → Actions
- Benjamin Franklin Assets/Liabilities method: weekly behavioral audit
- 1% daily improvement: compound = 37x over a year
- Leadership Avatar: define the leader you want to become. Daily calibration tool
- 6 Action Steps:
  1. Choose 1-2 mindsets and 1-2 skills to focus on
  2. Create your Leadership Avatar
  3. Practice Franklin method weekly
  4. Find accountability partners
  5. Seek feedback continuously
  6. Commit to 1% daily improvement
- Optimism as 10th element: foundational to all Notable Nine
