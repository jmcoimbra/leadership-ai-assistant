# 1:1 Meeting Protocol
> Owner: [Brain Owner] | Pillar: [Pillar N] | Status: Enforced | Last Audit: [YYYY-MM-DD]

## Purpose

1:1s are the highest-impact leadership activity. They are where delegation maturity advances, feedback is delivered, growth goals are tracked, and trust is built. This protocol ensures every 1:1 produces value, not just conversation.

## Before Every 1:1 (5 min prep)

1. Open the person's file in `09_people/`
2. Review: last 1:1 topics, open action items, growth goal progress
3. Feed file to AI with prompt: "Generate 3 suggested topics for this 1:1 based on: recent action items, growth goal status, and delegation maturity. Flag any concerns."
4. Self-check: "Am I about to give this person a task, or develop them?" If just task assignment, add a development topic.
5. Prepare at least 1 specific piece of feedback (positive or constructive) to deliver

## During the 1:1

1. **Start with their agenda, not yours.** Ask: "What's on your mind?"
2. **Give at least one piece of specific feedback.** Format: "[Specific behavior] → [Impact] → [Continue/Change]"
3. **Ask growth-oriented questions:**
   - "What do you need from me to unblock yourself?"
   - "What are you learning right now?"
   - "What decision did you make this week that you are proud of?"
   - "What would you do differently if you could redo something this week?"
4. **Check delegation maturity:** Are they making decisions you used to make? If not, why?
5. **Check energy:** "What is draining you? What is energizing you?"
6. **Purpose framing:** "What is the purpose behind your current work? Does it connect to where you want to be in 6 months?" Use this to test if assignments are purpose-aligned for the person, not just task completion.

## After Every 1:1 (5 min)

1. Log the 1:1 in their `09_people/` file within 24 hours — date, key topics, decisions, action items, follow-up status
2. Update action items — mark completed, add new
3. If feedback was given: note it in Performance Observations
4. If delegation opportunity was identified: note it in Delegation Maturity section
5. If energy/burnout signal detected: create specific action plan

## Cadence

Define per direct report in their `09_people/<name>.md` file. Default recommendations:

| Role | Recommended Cadence | Notes |
|------|---------------------|-------|
| Team lead | Weekly | Delegation maturity is critical |
| Senior IC | Bi-weekly | Check initiative ownership and AI adoption |
| Newer / ramping member | Weekly | Ramping needs more frequent touchpoints |
| Authority-building role | Weekly | Authority requires consistent reinforcement |

## Anti-Patterns

- 1:1 that is just status update → failed 1:1 (status belongs in standups/chat)
- 1:1 with zero feedback delivered → missed opportunity
- 1:1 not logged within 24 hours → brain decay
- 3 consecutive 1:1s with no growth-oriented discussion → you are managing, not leading
- Canceling 1:1s repeatedly → trust erosion signal

## Escalation

- If you skip logging a 1:1: force yourself to log within 48 hours. If this happens 3 times: the protocol is failing.
- If 3 consecutive 1:1s with someone have no feedback given: you are being too comfortable.
- If a 1:1 reveals burnout signals across 2 consecutive sessions: escalate to a dedicated conversation about workload/purpose alignment.

## AI Integration

Use AI to:
- Pre-generate 1:1 agendas from the person's `09_people/` file
- Identify patterns across 1:1 logs: are the same action items recurring? (indicates a systemic issue)
- Suggest delegation opportunities based on the person's growth trajectory
- Draft feedback based on recent performance observations
