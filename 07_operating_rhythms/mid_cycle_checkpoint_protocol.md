# Mid-Cycle Checkpoint Protocol
> Owner: [Brain Owner] | Pillar: [Pillar N] | Status: Active | Last Audit: [YYYY-MM-DD]

## Purpose

Development goals and career-trajectory milestones reference a mid-cycle checkpoint. This file defines exactly what that checkpoint looks like: what evidence to compile, how to self-assess, how to present to your manager, and what to do if checkpoints are not met.

## Quarterly Close Protocol

Lighter than mid-cycle but ensures a quarter does not close without a status sweep. No manager presentation needed — this is a self-audit.

### Quarterly Close Checklist (Start 3 days before quarter end)

- [ ] Update career evidence file with snapshot
- [ ] Audit career trajectory milestones: which are met, which missed?
- [ ] Audit development goals: are status blocks current?
- [ ] Run bi-weekly self-check
- [ ] Confirm AI baseline assessment is complete
- [ ] Check for stale files: any career/leadership file with Last Audit older than 2 weeks gets updated

### What Quarterly Close Should Produce

1. Updated career evidence with honest quarter snapshot
2. Career trajectory row updated with final evidence
3. Clear picture of what next quarter needs to deliver

### Escalation

If 3 days before quarter end arrives and you have not started: force-start. This takes 2 hours, not 2 weeks. No excuses.

## Mid-Cycle Timeline

Compress or expand based on review cadence. Default: 4 weeks of prep.

| When | Action |
|------|--------|
| Week 1 | Begin evidence compilation. Run self-assessment. |
| Week 2 | Complete evidence compilation. Draft manager presentation. |
| Week 3 | AI review of draft. Practice delivery. Finalize. |
| Week 4 | Deliver mid-cycle checkpoint in manager 1:1. |

**Non-negotiable:** This preparation starts in Week 1, not the day of. You need 3 weeks of compilation, not a last-minute scramble. Calendar-block these 4 weeks now.

## Manager Presentation Prep

### Structure (15-20 minutes max)

1. **Open with your self-score.** Do not wait for your manager to assess you — assess yourself first. This demonstrates self-awareness.

> "I prepared a mid-cycle checkpoint on the three goals you set. My self-assessment: [score]. Here's the evidence."

2. **Goal by goal: Evidence → Gap → Plan.** For each goal:

> "Goal [N]: [name]. Status: [met/in progress/not met]. Evidence: [2-3 specific data points]. Where I am still short: [honest gap]. My plan for the second half: [specific actions with dates]."

3. **Career trajectory update.** One competency you demonstrated. One that is still a gap.

4. **Ask for your manager's perspective.** This is the human-first moment.

> "That is my self-assessment. I want to hear yours. Where do you see me growing? Where do you see me still stuck?"

5. **Close with a commitment.**

> "Based on this conversation, here is what I am committing to for the second half: [1-3 specific deliverables with dates]. I will report back at [next checkpoint]."

### What NOT to Do

- **Do not present a 30-slide deck.** This is a conversation, not a presentation.
- **Do not over-explain failures.** State what happened, what you learned, and what you will do differently. Period.
- **Do not blame external factors.** Your manager does not care about constraints you did not escalate. If something blocked you and you did not flag it — that is on you.
- **Do not ask "how did I do?"** without first stating your own assessment. Self-awareness precedes feedback.

## If Checkpoints Are Not Met

### 0 of N goals at checkpoint: RED

Honest conversation with manager within the 1:1. Do not hide it. Then cancel or defer all non-essential work for 2 weeks. Each goal gets a specific 30-day sprint with daily progress tracking.

### Some goals at checkpoint: YELLOW/GREEN

Present the ones you met with evidence. Be direct about the ones that missed. Plan for the second half prioritizes the closest-to-checkpoint first.

### All goals at checkpoint: STRONG

Present the evidence. Then shift the conversation forward — what does the second half look like, and what can you take on that stretches you further?

## Escalation

- If evidence compilation reveals you have zero data for a goal: the data collection system failed. Escalate to yourself — dedicate the next 2 weeks exclusively to data capture.
- If self-assessment score is 2 or below: schedule an additional manager 1:1 specifically for course correction. Do not wait for the regular cadence.

## AI Integration

- **Evidence compilation:** Feed AI all source files. Prompt: "For each of my N development goals, compile the evidence that exists. For each, identify: strongest evidence, weakest evidence, and gaps. Be brutally honest."
- **Self-assessment review:** Feed AI your self-assessment draft. Prompt: "Is this honest or is it hedging? Score the honesty 1-5. Flag any language that sounds like excuse-making. Rewrite the gaps to be more direct."
- **Rehearsal:** Practice the manager presentation with AI playing your manager. The manager will ask hard follow-up questions. Practice staying direct, data-backed, and non-defensive.
