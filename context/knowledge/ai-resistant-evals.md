## Purpose

Design engineering evaluations that test judgment, verification, and collaboration with AI rather than memorized answers or hidden tool use.

## Rules

1. Require candidates to disclose AI use and explain which parts they delegated.
2. Include out-of-distribution follow-up questions that force reasoning beyond common training examples.
3. Grade process artifacts: prompts, verification steps, rejected options, tests, and final diff.
4. Penalize blind delegation when the candidate cannot explain or defend the output.
5. Reward calibrated AI use when the candidate catches tool errors and tightens the result.

## Escalation

If an evaluation can be completed by pasting the prompt into a generic model without candidate judgment, rewrite the exercise before reuse.
