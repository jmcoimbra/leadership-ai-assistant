## Behavioral Contract

The weekly review turns local brain state and verified external signals into a ranked operating agenda.

## Inputs

- Team scorecards.
- Project tracker.
- Team roster and individual development profiles.
- Career trajectory.
- Compliance program.
- Voice profile.
- Optional external systems exposed in the current agent session.

## Required Output

- Mode selected before loading broad context.
- Ranked topics with urgency, visibility, career impact, and pillar alignment.
- Execution risks with owner and deadline.
- Metric gaps with source and last-updated date.
- People follow-ups with cadence risk.
- Next-week plan with three priorities.
- Unverified claims labeled instead of asserted.

## Success Criteria

- No invented external facts.
- No stale metric cited as current when a newer source is available.
- Every action has owner, deadline, and escalation trigger.
- No outbound content contains private brain paths.

## Failure Conditions

- The review summarizes files without selecting actions.
- The review cites a source the agent did not verify.
- The review creates external communication without approval.
